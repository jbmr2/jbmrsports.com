Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

1. Introduction 2. Information we collect 3. How we use information 4. Third-party services 5. Data storage & retention 6. Your rights 7. Children 8. Contact

1. Introduction

JBMR Sports ("we", "us", "our") operates the JBMR Sports mobile apps (iOS and Android) and website at jbmrsports.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: mobile phone number when you sign in with OTP (optional; guest browsing is supported).
  • Usage data: matches you view, watch history, and watchlist stored on your device.
  • Downloads: ball clips and exported reels saved locally on your device.
  • Device & technical data: app version, device type, and network information needed to stream video and load live scores.

3. How we use information

  • Provide live cricket streaming, scorecards, highlights, and personalized watch features.
  • Maintain watch history and watchlist on your device.
  • Improve app performance, fix errors, and deliver updates.
  • Respond to support requests at support@jbmrsports.com.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for cross-app tracking.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — match schedules, highlights, and app content.
  • Cloudflare — live video streaming and media delivery.
  • CrickDB / cricket APIs — live scores and match statistics.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — app distribution.

5. Data storage & retention

  • Watch history and watchlist are stored locally on your device.
  • Downloaded clips remain on your device until you delete them.
  • Support emails are retained as long as needed to resolve your request.

6. Your rights

  • Clear local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.
  • Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of account data we hold.

7. Children

JBMR Sports is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data.

8. Contact

Questions? Email support@jbmrsports.com.
